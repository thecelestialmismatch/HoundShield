# The Weekly Report

Written 2026-09-08. Produced every Sunday by AUDITOR. This is the only thing
the system is required to show you. Everything else is internal.

## Rules for filling it in

1. Every number traces to Stripe, an inbox, or a signed document. Anything else
   is written as UNKNOWN, never estimated. An honest unknown is information; an
   estimate is a lie with a decimal point.
2. Drafted is not sent. Planned is not done. Live is not sold. Ready is not paid.
3. The section on what did not happen is not optional and is never left empty.
   A week with nothing in it means the week was not examined.
4. Maximum one page. If it does not fit, the work was unfocused, and the report
   says that rather than getting longer.

## The template

```

Days to cash checkpoint 2026-10-08:  [N]

MONEY

  Closed cumulative           $[N]

  Against 7,000 target        [N]%

CASH LANE
  Contacts made               [N]   (target 20/week)
  Replies                     [N]   ([N]% reply rate)
  Calls held                  [N]
  Proposals sent              [N]
  Closed                      [N]

ASSET LANE
  MSPs contacted              [N]   (target 100/week)
  Replies                     [N]   ([N]% reply rate)
  Calls booked                [N]
  Reports sold                [N]
  Cumulative contacts         [N]/400 before the offer is judged

WHAT SHIPPED
  [one line each, only things a stranger can now see or use]

WHAT DID NOT HAPPEN
  [one line each, and why, without softening]

DELETED THIS WEEK
  [SURGEON must list at least one thing]

THE ONE NUMBER THAT MOVED
  [the single most meaningful change, and what it means]

NEXT WEEK, ONE THING
  [a single action, not a list]

DECISION NEEDED FROM YOU
  [only genuine founder decisions, or "none"]
```

## How to read it

Three failure signatures, and each has a different fix. Confusing them is how
months disappear.

1. Reply rate under 2 percent. The message is wrong. Nobody is reaching the
   offer, so the offer has not been tested. Change the first line only.
2. Reply rate above 5 percent and no sales. The message works and the offer
   does not. People are interested until they see what is actually being sold.
   Change the offer, keep the message.
3. Sales but no usage or no renewal. The product is wrong. This is the best
   problem in the list because it means money already changed hands.

Under Law four, no conclusion is drawn from fewer than ten attempts, and only
one variable changes between samples.

## What a good week looks like

Not a full checklist. One number moved and one thing was deleted.

A week with 20 contacts, 1 reply, 0 dollars and one deleted roadmap is a good
week in month one. A week with a redesigned landing page, a new strategy
document and 0 contacts is a bad week regardless of how it felt, and the report
is required to say so in the section on what did not happen.
